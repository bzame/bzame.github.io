Case study2022

Imola Formula 1 Grand Prix: spectators and mobility

How many people attended the Grand Prix, where they came from and how they moved, from mobile network data.

Client
Autodromo Internazionale Enzo e Dino Ferrari (Imola circuit)
My role
Lead data scientist
Data
Mobile network data

The full case study, with its figures, is on Motion Analytica's site. This page is my side of it: the question, what I did, what I learned.

Case study on Motion Analytica's site Italian version

The question

A Formula 1 Grand Prix brings tens of thousands of people to a small city in a few days. The circuit wanted to know how many spectators came, from where, and how they reached and left Imola.

What I did

  • Collected the technical requirements.
  • Ran the analyses.

The study later became a paper by colleagues at Motion Analytica and the University of Bologna.

What I remember

A second phase, with a deeper analysis of mobility, was planned. It never happened: the 2023 Grand Prix was called off because of the floods in Emilia-Romagna, and the project was cancelled with it.
