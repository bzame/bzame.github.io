Case study2018

Padua: travel in the city and its hinterland

Motion Analytica's first origin–destination study: trips between Padua and its hinterland for the city's sustainable mobility plan.

Client
Comune di Padova (City of Padua)
My role
Data scientist
Data
Vodafone mobile network data, other sources

The full case study, with its figures, is on Motion Analytica's site. This page is my side of it: the question, what I did, what I learned.

Case study on Motion Analytica's site Italian version

The question

Padua was preparing its sustainable urban mobility plan (PUMS) and a new tram line, and needed to know how people move between the city and the surrounding towns. It was Motion Analytica’s first origin–destination (O-D) project.

What I did

  • Met the city’s technical staff to collect the requirements: the analysis zones and how detailed the results had to be.
  • Developed the first O-D algorithm, from mobile network events to trips between zones, and made it production-ready: later studies built on it.
  • Built the dashboards that delivered the results.

What I remember

The city’s zoning existed only on paper. We rebuilt the zones in digital form, starting from Istat census areas and drawing part of the boundaries by hand.
