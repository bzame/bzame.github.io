Project page on Motion Analytica's site (Italian)

The question

Who travels through a passenger port, where do they come from and go to, and how do they reach it: by rail, from an airport, by road? A proof of concept, funded by the region, for a new line of analytics on port mobility.

What I did

  • Co-wrote the application, for the technical part.
  • Supervised the implementation of the prototype: choosing the data sources, collecting the port calls (each ship’s arrival and departure), and the algorithm that fuses mobile network data with the port calls.
  • Co-wrote the final report.

What I remember

We started from the analogies with our aviation product, built for the Milan airports. Some things turned out much easier: ships enter and leave a port far less often than planes take off and land. Others were harder: mobile network coverage in a port is not like an airport’s. That made the project more interesting than we expected.
