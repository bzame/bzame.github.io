Case study2019–ongoing

FS Italiane: rail and mobility analytics from mobile network data

A long-running programme for the Italian railways group: from the first deliveries to algorithms, monthly bulletins and joint papers.

Client
Ferservizi (FS Italiane group), for the FS Research Centre
Partners
Vodafone (contractor; today Fastweb + Vodafone)
My role
Project leader, then scientific reviewer and advisor
Data
Mobile network data

The question

How do people travel across Italy, by rail and otherwise, and how many use each station? The FS group measures it continuously with mobile network data, alongside its own data.

Ferservizi awarded the work to Vodafone after two public tenders, won by Vodafone with Motion Analytica as subcontractor. There is no public case study; some of the outputs are published by FS Italiane’s Research Centre and are listed at the end of this page.

What I did

  • At the start, project leader: designed and implemented, with the team, the first deliveries.
  • Then evolved the algorithms and the range of analyses.
  • Today I am the project’s institutional memory and its scientific reviewer, including for the joint papers with FS. The project has matured into a standard service, with less frequent methodological changes.

What I remember

The periodic moments when parts of the method, sometimes large ones, were put into question. With FS we would review definitions and analytical strategies in detail when new evidence came up, for example data that at first could not be used in the process.
