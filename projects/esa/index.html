ESA RACE: COVID-19 and farm workers in Foggia

Mobile network data and satellite imagery together: farm workers' movements and the tomato harvest in the province of Foggia during the pandemic.

Client
European Space Agency (RACE dashboard)
Partners
Vodafone Italia
My role
Data scientist
Data
Satellite imagery, mobile network data

The full case study, with its figures, is on Motion Analytica's site. This page is my side of it: the question, what I did, what I learned.

Case study on Motion Analytica's site Italian version

The question

During the pandemic, ESA’s Rapid Action on COVID-19 and EO (RACE) dashboard tracked the effects of the crisis seen from space. In the province of Foggia the question was whether the restrictions changed the presence of farm workers during the tomato harvest, comparing the summers of 2019 and 2020.

What I did

  • Collected the requirements.
  • Coordinated the data science team for the data processing and the dashboards.

What I remember

An unexpected meeting of two very different phenomena: people’s movements, seen through the mobile network, and the growth of tomato crops, seen from space.
