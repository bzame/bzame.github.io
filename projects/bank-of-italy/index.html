Bank of Italy: mobile network data for the balance of payments

Foreign travellers in Italy measured with mobile network data, for the "travel" item of the balance of payments.

Client
Bank of Italy (Banca d'Italia)
My role
Project leader
Data
Mobile network data

The question

The “travel” item of Italy’s balance of payments records what foreign visitors spend in Italy and what Italians spend abroad. It rests on counting travellers, and the Bank of Italy has been studying how mobile network data can make those counts more accurate and more timely.

A Motion Analytica project. There is no public case study, but the Bank has published its own papers on the method and its results, listed at the end of this page.

What I did

  • Collected the requirements.
  • Managed the Motion Analytica team.
  • Coordinated the regular working sessions with the Bank on methodology, science and analytical results.
  • Was responsible for the quality of the results.

What I remember

The Bank’s prudence in adopting new data and new methods. Every result was checked with constant methodological and scientific rigour, including its continuity and consistency with the existing series.
