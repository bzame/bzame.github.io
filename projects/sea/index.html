Case study2020–2025

Milan airports: who flies from Linate and Malpensa

From a first analysis of an airport's passengers to a product combining mobile network data, official statistics and flight schedules.

Client
SEA Aeroporti di Milano (Milan Airports)
Partners
Vodafone Italia
My role
Lead data scientist for the first analysis and the first versions of the product
Data
Vodafone mobile network data, airport data, official statistics, flight schedules

The full case study, with its figures, is on Motion Analytica's site. This page is my side of it: the question, what I did, what I learned.

Case study on Motion Analytica's site Italian version

The question

Who flies from Linate and Malpensa, where do they really come from and go to, and do they fly within Italy or abroad? Airports know their passenger counts, not their passengers’ true origins or catchment areas.

What I did

  • Designed the first analysis of an airport’s mobility patterns.
  • Then designed the first versions of the complete product: a data fusion of mobile network data with official statistics and actual flight schedules, giving a full picture of air travel in Italy.

What I remember

Watching a one-off analysis with few details grow into a consolidated, well-structured product.
