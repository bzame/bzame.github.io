How it started

In 2016 the region commissioned a study of travel flows across its territory, for transport planning. It was carried out by CS Research, the research group Motion Analytica grew out of, and it laid the foundations for the work with the region that followed.

The work today

Motion Analytica now works on several lines in the region:

  1. Trieste Trasporti (since 2026): mobility analyses for the local public transport company, in partnership with the University of Trieste (DEAMS department).
  2. Regione Friuli Venezia Giulia, mobility department (since 2025): analyses that fed into the regional transport plan (PGT 2025).
  3. PromoTurismoFVG (since 2025): tourism across the region.
  4. Trieste Airport (since 2024).

I am Motion Analytica’s project leader for the first three.
