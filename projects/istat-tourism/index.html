Case study2020–2024

Istat: tourism statistics from mobile network data

With Istat and Vodafone Business, methods to measure tourism from mobile network data, from experiments to papers and a product feature.

Client
Istat (Italian National Institute of Statistics)
Partners
Vodafone Business
My role
Lead data scientist
Data
Vodafone mobile network data

The full case study, with its figures, is on Motion Analytica's site. This page is my side of it: the question, what I did, what I learned.

Case study on Motion Analytica's site Italian version

The question

Can mobile network data give official tourism statistics that are more timely and more detailed than surveys alone, and still meet the standards of a national statistics institute? Istat and Vodafone Business explored it on real cases, such as Rome and Rimini.

What I did

  • Represented Motion Analytica in the working discussions with Istat.
  • Designed the algorithms and both the intermediate and the final analyses.
  • Turned the results into scientific papers and into new analytics modules: the usual environment (the places a person visits routinely, so that trips beyond it count as tourism) became a feature of Motion Analytica’s standard tourism analytics product.

What I remember

Istat’s statisticians were demanding and, at the same time, very welcoming. The collaboration continues: we keep in touch, and it led to invitations to events (the MNO-MINDS conference) and to joint publications.
