The question

Tourism leaves traces in many data sources, and each tells only part of the story. BD4T (“Metodologie per la lettura integrata di Big Data a favore di una comprensione olistica dei fenomeni turistici”) developed methods to read them together, for a holistic picture of tourism.

What I did

  • Co-wrote the application, for the technical part.
  • Supervised the implementation of the prototype.
  • Co-wrote the final report and the poster presented at the closing event.

What I remember

It was the first use in the field of the usual environment method we had developed with Istat. It produced much more detailed statistics, daily instead of monthly and below the municipal level, and we still checked with Ca’ Foscari that the results held up.
