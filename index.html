Bruno Zamengo

Head of Research at Motion Analytica: mobility and tourism insights from mobile network data.

I’m a statistician and data scientist. At Motion Analytica I lead research on mobility big data: methods that turn mobile network, GPS and transport data into statistics on how people move and travel, developed with universities, Istat and the Bank of Italy. I have worked on more than a hundred studies for public bodies, transport operators and tourism destinations, and I lead publicly funded research projects from the application to delivery.
