Bruno Zamengo

Head of Research · Motion Analytica · Venice, Italy

Statistician and data scientist. At Motion Analytica I lead research on mobility big data (mobile network operator (MNO), GPS and transport data): new data sources, algorithms and methods that turn raw traces into insights and statistics on mobility and tourism, built with universities and national statistical offices. I have worked on more than a hundred studies for public bodies, transport operators and tourism destinations, first as a data scientist and then leading the data science team. I also lead publicly funded research projects (EU regional development and NextGenerationEU programmes), from the application to delivery.
