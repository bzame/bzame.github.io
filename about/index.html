About

I’m a statistician from the Venice area. I studied Statistics and IT, then Statistical Science, at the University of Padua, and I have been turning data into answers ever since: first about supermarket customers at Gruppo PAM, then, from 2018, about how people move, at Motion Analytica, where I now lead research.

Most of my work starts from mobile network data: anonymous signals that, with the right methods, become statistics on mobility and tourism. The part I enjoy most is the one in between: listening to what a city, a destination, a company or a statistics office actually needs, then finding a method that still holds when experts challenge it. Working with Istat, the Bank of Italy and universities taught me how much rigour that takes. Some of the projects and the papers that came out of them are on this site.

I also like explaining it: at conferences, and to people who don’t speak “data”. How people learn, and how a product can make something complex feel simple, are two things I keep coming back to.

About this site

I’ve had personal pages online since 2017, hand-written and rarely updated. This version is a complete rework, done in 2026 with Claude, Anthropic’s AI assistant: we started by building a small visual identity (monogram, palette, type and chart rules), then reorganised the content and the structure, and rebuilt the site with Hugo. The CV, the publications and the projects are generated from one set of data files, so they stay in step.
